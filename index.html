Where it comes from

MAPD production bonus tiers

The tier you reach pays that rate on all of your MAPD enrollments for the week (a double deal counts as 2). Under 10 enrollments, no MAPD production pay. The double-deal bonus and the $10 attachment bonus are only paid once you reach the 10-enrollment tier. Fronting pay needs 10 fronted enrollments in the week.

This is a gross estimate before taxes and withholdings. Ancillary pay is the first month’s premium on active, effectuated policies and is subject to a 90-day chargeback; MAPD enrollments that don’t go into effect are charged back. Contracts are paid after they become payable, so amounts shown here may land on a later paycheck.
